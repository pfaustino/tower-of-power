<p><strong>Tower of Power</strong> is a browser-based 3D tower defense game. Hold the crystal outpost against escalating UFO invasions — place towers, upgrade them under fire, and spend power crystals on emergency abilities when the swarm breaks through.</p>

<h2>Defend the Outpost</h2>
<p>UFOs pour in for up to <strong>100 waves</strong> per map. Build a line of fire, keep towers repaired when enemies return fire, and stop leaks before they drain the outpost. Clear <strong>wave 10</strong> to unlock the next battlefield.</p>

<h2>Ten Maps</h2>
<p>From the tutorial L-bend of <strong>Crystal Outpost</strong> to the endgame courtyards of <strong>Final Bastion</strong> — each map reshapes how you place and prioritize.</p>
<ul>
  <li>Crystal Outpost · Dust Ridge · North Pass · Crosswind Valley · Broken Corridor</li>
  <li>Shard Pocket · Wide Frontier · Helix Run · Long March · Final Bastion</li>
</ul>
<p>Between waves you can jump back to <strong>Maps</strong>, or <strong>Continue</strong> from your last cleared wave with crystal refunds for hand and tower spend.</p>

<h2>Towers</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Needle Spire (1)</strong> — fast single-target bolts</li>
  <li><strong>Boulder Keep (2)</strong> — ballistic splash on impact</li>
  <li><strong>Scorch Spire (3)</strong> — ignited oil burn zones</li>
  <li><strong>Pulse Turret (4)</strong> — rapid pulses that slow swarms</li>
</ul>
<p>Upgrade towers up to level 10, repair damage mid-siege, sell for a refund, or <strong>Repair All</strong> between waves. Select a tower to set its <strong>target priority</strong>: Closest, Lowest HP, or Highest HP.</p>

<h2>Power Abilities</h2>
<p>Spend crystals on one-shot powers when the lane gets ugly:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Q — Freeze</strong> — instant lane freeze (no aim)</li>
  <li><strong>W — Strike</strong> — aim and click to drop an orbital strike</li>
  <li><strong>E — Overclock</strong> — overcharge every tower for a burst of firepower</li>
</ul>

<h2>Bosses &amp; Difficulty</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Boss waves every 10th wave</strong> with telegraphs and bonus crystal loot</li>
  <li>Five difficulties: <strong>Casual · Normal · Veteran · Hard · Nightmare</strong></li>
  <li>Local leaderboard plus optional global submissions (victories / waves cleared)</li>
</ul>

<h2>Controls</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>1–4</strong> — select tower type · <strong>click</strong> to place / select</li>
  <li><strong>Q / W / E</strong> — Freeze / Strike / Overclock</li>
  <li><strong>Esc</strong> — pause &amp; settings (audio, lighting, difficulty)</li>
  <li><strong>Pinch or on-screen +/-</strong> — zoom on mobile</li>
</ul>

<h2>Play in Browser</h2>
<p>No download required — hit <strong>Run game</strong>. Best on desktop; mobile is supported with on-screen zoom.</p>
